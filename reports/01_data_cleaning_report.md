# Data Cleaning Report

Generated by `src/data_cleaning.py` from `Tetuan City power consumption (UCI original).csv`.

## Dataset
**Power Consumption of Tetouan City**: UCI Machine Learning Repository (dataset 849). Three urban electricity distribution networks in Tetouan, northern Morocco (Quads / Smir / Boussafou substations, operator Amendis), recorded every 10 minutes through 2017, with five weather channels. Zones are labelled Zone 1 / Zone 2 / Zone 3. Source paper: Salam & El Hibaoui (2018), IEEE IRSEC.

## Provenance note
This project began from a modified copy of the dataset: the three zones had been renamed to college-block labels, the year relabelled 2017 -> 2023 (the two years share an identical weekday calendar), ~9% of timestamp strings were corrupted by a faulty export (minutes as a running counter, e.g. `0:60`, `0:2591`), and ~150 power values (0.1% of cells, magnitude < 0.05%) were altered. The pipeline now reads the pristine UCI original, and all models and metrics reported elsewhere in this project were refitted on this cleaned output. See `reports/05_modeling_report.md` for the separate modelling defects found in the same audit and the corrected results.
